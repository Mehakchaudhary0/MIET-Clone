About Us

29 Years of Excellence in Education, Innovation, and Leadership

Meerut Institute of Engineering & Technology (MIET), Meerut, established in 1997, is one of North India's leading institutions for higher education, dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, industry-oriented programs, experienced faculty, and a strong focus on research and entrepreneurship, MIET empowers students to become skilled professionals and responsible leaders.
