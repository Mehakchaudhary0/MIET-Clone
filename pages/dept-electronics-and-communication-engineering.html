Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)

ECE provides a broad foundation in electronics and communication with opportunities in communication, signal processing, embedded systems, VLSI, automation, robotics, drone technology, IoT and AI/ML.

HEAD OF DEPARTMENT

HoD's Message

Our department is committed to academic excellence, practical learning, innovation, research and the overall professional development of students. We encourage students to build strong fundamentals, participate in projects and activities, and prepare for higher education and industry.

01

Vision

To be an outstanding department imparting need-based, value-based technical education and producing socially responsible, self-reliant technocrats.

02

Mission

  • Educate students in core and allied ECE areas with state-of-the-art resources.
  • Imbibe outcome-based and value-based education for holistic development.
  • Create an innovative environment for socio-economic and environmental solutions.
