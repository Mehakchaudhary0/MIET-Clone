A Message from Our Placement Director

At MIET, we believe in empowering students with the skills, knowledge and confidence to thrive in a dynamic professional world.

The Training and Placement Cell works to bridge the gap between academia and industry, ensuring students are prepared to meet the expectations of leading organizations.

MIET continues to build strong industry partnerships and facilitate successful career opportunities for its future-ready students while welcoming recruiters to engage with its talent pool.
